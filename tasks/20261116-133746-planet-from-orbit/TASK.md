# Voxel Planet stage 8: Things to find from orbit: caps, city lights by kind, a moon, re-entry

- STATUS: OPEN
- PRIORITY: 272
- TAGS: godot,demo,planet,0.1.9

The research and its plan: `work/research/planet-2026-11-16.md`; the parent task is `tasks/20261116-133746-planet`.

From orbit the first demo shows continents and oceans, belts of mountains, deserts and forests by
the climate, polar caps of snow and sea ice, the cloud layer's weather, the sun's glint on the
sea, a settled region's glow at night, the day's edge, and the rim of the air with its sunsets.
What is left, as the owner finds it wanting: the lights of each town by its kind, an aurora seen
from above as a ring round the pole, a moon to fly to, a glow on the board on the way back into
the air, rings, a second planet.
