## OpenBLAS (the `openblas` package)

`demos/python/Dollyfile-openblas`: OpenBLAS 0.3.34's release archive, built by its CMake project
with `-DARCH=wasm -DBINARY=64 -DTARGET=GENERIC -DNOFORTRAN=1 -DC_LAPACK=1 -DNO_LAPACKE=1
-DUSE_THREAD=0`, static; `pkg-config openblas` gives `-I/usr/include/openblas` and `-lopenblas`.

Decisions, each measured in a session (`build/scipy-evidence/explore.mjs`, commands and outputs
beside it):

- No claim of Emscripten. OpenBLAS knows wasm only under `__EMSCRIPTEN__` (`ctest.c`, which then
  also prints `OS_WINDOWS`), and its wasm kernels (`kernel/wasm`, `WASM128_GENERIC`) are SIMD128.
  Its CMake project takes the architecture as configuration: `ARCH` is a cache variable that
  `cmake/system_check.cmake` only defaults (it warns "Target ARCH could not be determined, got
  wasm64"), `BINARY` too (it would default to 32 for an architecture it does not list). With
  `TARGET=GENERIC` the kernels are `kernel/wasm/KERNEL`'s generic C set, and no `-msimd128`.
- CMake, not the Makefiles: the Makefile build needs `ctest.c` patched (no `ARCH_WASM` without
  Emscripten), `Makefile.wasm` patched (it adds `-msimd128` for every core), and archives with
  `ar -ru`, which Dolly's `ar` refuses (`only deterministic archive updates with r, q or s`; GNU
  and LLVM `ar` accept `u` and ignore it in deterministic mode). `c_check` also runs `cc -S`,
  which Dolly's `cc` does not take. CMake archives with Dolly's platform rule (`ar rcs`).
- `openblas-dolly.patch` (proposed upstream, not filed), three changes, its head says why:
  `kernel/CMakeLists.txt` reads `KERNEL.generic` only if it exists, as the Makefile's `-include`
  does; `param.h` gives GENERIC on wasm the cache blocking `WASM128_GENERIC` has; and LAPACK's
  subroutines return void. The last is most of the patch (743 lines of 188 files, generated by
  rule): OpenBLAS defines the LAPACK routines it implements (`getrf`, `getrs`, `gesv`, `potrf`,
  `lauum`, `trtri`, `laswp`, `laed3` and their unblocked forms) and 42 files of its C translation
  (`larft`, `larf1f`, `gedmd`, `geqp3rk`...) as returning `int`, and declares some of them `int`
  and others `void` where it calls them; SciPy declares every subroutine `void`. WebAssembly
  checks a call's type, and wasm-ld links a mismatched call to a trap: before the patch OpenBLAS's
  own `utest` trapped at `potrf:bug_695`, my check program's `dgesv_` trapped, and linking every
  object of the library warned of 108 mismatched symbols (`dgeqrf_` calls `dlarft_` as void).
  After it: no mismatch, `utest` 124 of 124 and `utest_ext` 1535 of 1535 pass. (xerbla_ stays
  `int`, as its 1035 declarations say; the 3.12 routines also called it without its length.)
- `NO_LAPACKE`: nothing in Dolly uses LAPACK's C interface; SciPy declares its own prototypes.
- Size: `libopenblas.a` 10.6 MB; the package's snapshot 11.1 MB, its packs 3.0 MB compressed.
  Built in 1.5 min in a session (`-j8`), 341 s as an image in a `bigbuild` slot.
- Tested by python-data's demo test: a session of `cc`, `pkgconf` and `openblas` links
  `openblas-check.c` through `pkg-config openblas` and runs it (a product, a solve, a Cholesky and
  a QR of known results, the QR through `dlarft_`, which trapped before), Chromium 10.1 s and
  Firefox 11.5 s; `amy-browser.mjs` names the demo as where the package is used.

Speed (session, Chromium, `demos/python/test/fixtures/openblas-check.c`): DGEMM 500 x 500
0.024 s (10.2 GFLOP/s), 1000 x 1000 0.19 s (10.4-10.7 GFLOP/s); DGESV 500 0.009 s, 1000 0.065 s.
